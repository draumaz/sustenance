Sustenance is a private, open-source window into the nutrition and energy data stored
in Android's Health Connect. There are no accounts, ads, or trackers.

Features:

* Today dashboard, Material You cards for nutrition and energy, each with an
  at-a-glance sparkline. Pull up to look back at previous days.
* Detail views, tap any metric for a full chart, statistics and recent records.
* Weekly summary, daily averages measured against goals you set, with progress rings
  and week-over-week trends.
* Fasting timer, see how long since you last ate, with an optional notification when
  you reach your goal.
* Home-screen widget, your key numbers, themed to match your wallpaper.
* Optional food logging, add your own Google AI Studio API key and snap a photo of a
  meal or nutrition label. Gemini estimates the nutrients, explains anything it had to
  guess, and you review and edit everything before it's saved to Health Connect.

Sustenance reads and writes through Health Connect, so it works alongside whatever apps
and wearables already use it. You stay in control of exactly which data types it can
access, and can change or revoke access at any time from Health Connect.

Your data stays on your device. The only exception is food logging: when you use it,
the photos and any notes you add are sent to Google's Gemini API using your own key.
